# Content Rules

Denizign copy should feel:

- clear
- concise
- intelligent
- human
- confident
- professional

Always follow the source hierarchy defined in `/CLAUDE.md`.

Always follow the positioning in `/brand/brand-foundation.md`.

## Tone

Use direct language.

Prefer concrete statements over marketing language.

Avoid:

- exaggerated claims
- buzzwords
- vague creative language
- unnecessary adjectives
- generic AI copy

Prefer:

"Complex systems. Clear experiences."

over:

"I create innovative, cutting-edge and unforgettable digital experiences."

## Positioning

The portfolio primarily positions Deniz as:

- UX Lead
- Product Experience professional
- Systems thinker

His frontend background, product ownership and technical knowledge support that positioning.

Do not position Deniz primarily as:

- graphic designer
- web designer
- frontend developer
- creative agency

## Case studies

Case studies should explain reasoning.

Use the narrative:

**Problem → Thinking → Intervention → Outcome**

Show:

- context
- complexity
- decisions
- trade-offs
- role
- design reasoning
- outcome
- reflection

Do not create unexplained galleries of polished screens.

## Claims

Never invent:

- research findings
- metrics
- user numbers
- business impact
- responsibilities
- outcomes
- quotations

When verified information is missing, use an explicit placeholder or request the information.

## Headlines

Prefer short, strong headlines.

One main idea per section.

Avoid long marketing headlines.

## Body copy

Keep paragraphs readable and relatively concise.

Use plain language where possible.

The portfolio should demonstrate expertise without sounding unnecessarily complicated.
