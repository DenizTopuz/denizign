# Denizign Imagery

Imagery should support the Denizign brand concept:

From complexity to clarity.

Images are not decoration.
They should help explain the work, provide atmosphere or strengthen the story.

---

## Product imagery

Product design work is the most important visual material.

Prefer:

- real interface designs
- high-quality UI screenshots
- map interfaces
- dashboards
- data visualisations
- flows
- prototypes
- design systems
- interaction details
- research artefacts where relevant

UI should preferably be shown large.

Avoid placing every interface inside device mockups.

The work itself should be the visual focus.

---

## Case study imagery

Images should help explain the story.

Use imagery to show:

- complexity before simplification
- information architecture
- user flows
- design decisions
- iterations
- final interfaces
- systems and relationships

Do not create galleries of unexplained screenshots.

Every important image should have a purpose.

---

## Photography

Portrait photography should feel:

- editorial
- calm
- confident
- professional
- architectural
- natural

Preferred characteristics:

- natural or controlled soft light
- neutral environments
- strong composition
- negative space
- restrained colour
- subtle contrast

Architecture, concrete, glass, structure and clean spaces can support the visual identity.

---

## Avoid

Do not use:

- generic stock photography
- staged laptop and coffee photography
- fake office collaboration scenes
- cliché UX sticky-note photography
- random 3D shapes
- colourful abstract blobs
- generic AI-generated technology imagery
- neon futuristic visuals
- excessive device mockups

---

## Image treatment

Keep imagery clean.

Preferred:

- natural colour
- slightly restrained saturation
- strong neutral tones
- consistent image ratios
- generous surrounding whitespace

Do not add decorative gradients or heavy overlays.

---

## Principle

Product imagery explains.

Photography adds personality.

Visualisation clarifies complexity.

Decoration should remain minimal.
