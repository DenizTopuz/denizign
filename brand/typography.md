# Denizign Typography

Typography is one of the strongest visual elements of the Denizign identity.

The website uses a deliberate contrast between bold expressive headlines and restrained readable body typography.

---

## Display typography

Font:
Manrope

Source:
Google Fonts

Preferred weights:

800 ExtraBold
700 Bold
600 SemiBold

Use Manrope for:

- hero headlines
- section headlines
- project titles
- important statements

---

## Body typography

Font:
Inter

Source:
Google Fonts

Preferred weights:

400 Regular
500 Medium
600 SemiBold

Use Inter for:

- body copy
- navigation
- buttons
- labels
- metadata
- UI

---

## Typography character

Headlines should feel:

Bold.
Confident.
Minimal.
Editorial.

Body copy should feel:

Quiet.
Readable.
Neutral.
Human.

## Rules

Use short headlines.

Avoid long centered paragraphs.

Body copy should generally remain between approximately 55–72 characters per line.

Do not use multiple decorative fonts.

Do not introduce additional typefaces without explicit approval.
