# Denizign Colors

The Denizign color system is intentionally minimal.

The identity consists primarily of:

- warm white
- near-black
- neutral grey
- red accent

Red is the only strong brand accent.

---

## Primary

### Ink

#111214

Primary typography.
Dark backgrounds.
Strong contrast.

Never replace this with pure #000000 unless technically necessary.

---

## Graphite

#34373B

Use for:

- secondary dark surfaces
- subtle variation within dark compositions

It should support Near Black, not compete with it.

---

## Background

### Warm white

#FAFAF8

Default page background.

The website should feel slightly warmer than pure white.

---

## Surface

#FFFFFF

Cards, overlays and content surfaces.

---

## Soft Surface

#F1F0EC

Use for:

- subtle hover backgrounds
- secondary neutral surfaces
- low-emphasis visual grouping

---

## Secondary text

#6E7177

Supporting copy and metadata.

---

## Light Ink

#B9BBC0

Use for:

- secondary text on dark backgrounds
- quiet interface information in dark sections

Do not use it for primary body text on light backgrounds.

---

## Border

#E2E2DE

Subtle dividers, borders and UI structure.

---

## Accent

### Denizign Red

#D7263D

Primary brand accent.

Use for:

- selected text
- small highlights
- active states
- primary actions
- links
- meaningful visual accents
- the Denizign logo where appropriate

---

## Accent dark

#B91C32

Hover and active states.

---

## Accent soft

#FDE8EA

Very subtle red backgrounds.

Use sparingly.

---

## Red Very Soft

#FFF4F5

Use for:

- very subtle brand highlights
- quiet red-tinted backgrounds

Use sparingly.

---

## Color ratio

As a general visual guideline:

70% neutral light
20% dark / graphite
10% red maximum

Red should attract attention because it is rare.

---

## Do not

Do not introduce:

- gradients
- additional brand colors
- blue accents
- purple accents
- green accents
- colorful decorative sections

unless explicitly approved.

The minimal color system is a deliberate part of the Denizign identity.
